# Hestra Elevators Website - Master Prompt

Act as an **Expert UI/UX Designer, Senior React.js Frontend Developer, Creative Director, and SEO Specialist**. Build a **premium, modern, responsive, production-ready corporate website** for **Hestra Elevators**, a company specializing in elevator manufacturing, installation, modernization, maintenance, repair, Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), and safety inspections. The website should create trust, showcase engineering excellence, generate customer enquiries, and communicate the company's commitment to quality, safety, reliability, and innovation.

Before designing the website, analyze leading elevator company websites such as **Otis, KONE, Schindler, TK Elevator, Mitsubishi Electric, Hitachi, Fujitec, and Hyundai Elevator**. Do not copy any existing design. Instead, create a completely original website inspired by their layout, professionalism, content hierarchy, and user experience. The final design should feel comparable to premium global engineering brands like **Apple, Tesla, Siemens, ABB, Bosch, and Schneider Electric**, with a unique identity for Hestra Elevators.

Build the website using **React.js (JavaScript only), Vite, React Router DOM, SCSS, Framer Motion, GSAP (optional), React Icons, Swiper.js, CSS Grid, and Flexbox**. Do not use Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, Material UI, or TypeScript. Follow a clean, scalable folder structure with reusable components, SCSS variables, mixins, utility functions, reusable data files, semantic HTML, and production-ready coding standards.

The overall website should have a **premium, minimal, elegant, and engineering-focused design** with generous white space, large typography, soft shadows, rounded corners, smooth animations, and high-quality imagery. The design language should be inspired by the uploaded reference screenshots, using a sophisticated monochrome theme rather than bright colors.

Use the following color palette throughout the website:

* Background: **#F7F7F7**
* Section Background: **#F1F1F1**
* Card Background: **#FFFFFF**
* Primary Text: **#111111**
* Secondary Text: **#5F5F5F**
* Muted Text: **#8B8B8B**
* Border: **#E5E5E5**
* Accent Color: **Steel Blue (#2F5D7C)**

Use the accent color only for buttons, active navigation, links, icons, hover states, and interactive elements. Avoid colorful gradients and maintain a clean monochrome corporate appearance. Use **Poppins** for headings and **Inter** for body text.

Design the navigation bar exactly like the uploaded reference image. It should have a transparent background initially, become sticky while scrolling, and change to a white background with a subtle shadow after scrolling. The navigation should be perfectly aligned with generous left and right padding, a centered layout, and evenly spaced navigation items. Include the following menu items: **Home, About, Products, Services, Industries, Projects, Safety & Quality, Maintenance, Blog, Careers, Contact, and Request Quote**. The navigation should have smooth underline hover animations and responsive behavior with a hamburger menu on mobile devices.

Create a full-screen hero section with a premium elevator-related background image or looping video showing modern glass elevators, luxury commercial buildings, or high-rise structures. Add a subtle dark overlay to improve text readability. Center the hero content both vertically and horizontally, similar to the uploaded reference image. Include a bold heading such as **"Moving People Safely. Elevating Every Building."**, a supporting description about elevator manufacturing, installation, modernization, and maintenance, and two call-to-action buttons: **Get Free Quote** and **Explore Products**. Below the hero, display animated counters showing **500+ Projects Completed, 100+ Happy Clients, 15+ Years of Experience, and 24×7 Customer Support**.

Create dedicated pages for **Home, About Us, Products, Services, Industries, Projects, Safety & Quality, Maintenance (AMC), Gallery, Blog, Careers, FAQs, Contact Us, Request Quote, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and a custom 404 page**.

The Home page should include the following sections:

* Hero Section
* About Hestra Elevators
* Elevator Products
* Services
* Industries We Serve
* Why Choose Hestra Elevators
* Safety & Quality
* Installation Process
* Maintenance & AMC Plans
* Projects Showcase
* Testimonials
* Certifications
* Frequently Asked Questions
* Contact Section
* Premium Footer

Under the Products section, showcase various elevator solutions including **Passenger Elevators, Home Elevators, Hospital Elevators, Freight Elevators, Automobile Elevators, Capsule Elevators, Hydraulic Elevators, Machine Room Less Elevators (MRL), Observation Elevators, Dumbwaiters, and Goods Lifts**. Each product should include a professional image, description, key features, specifications, applications, capacity, speed, and a Learn More button.

The Services section should highlight **Design, Manufacturing, Installation, Modernization, Repair, Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), Preventive Maintenance, Emergency Breakdown Service, Spare Parts, Safety Inspections, Testing, and Commissioning**.

Include an Industries section showcasing the company's experience in **Residential Buildings, Commercial Complexes, Hospitals, Hotels, Shopping Malls, Airports, Metro Stations, Government Buildings, Educational Institutions, Warehouses, Factories, and IT Parks**.

Create a strong **Safety & Quality** section explaining the company's commitment to elevator safety, including **Load Testing, Brake Testing, Door Sensor Testing, Emergency Alarm Systems, Automatic Rescue Devices, Electrical Safety, Fire Safety Compliance, Overspeed Governors, Buffer Testing, Child Safety Features, Power Backup Systems, Earthquake Protection (where applicable), and Multi-Level Quality Inspections**. Present this information using premium process cards, icons, timelines, and illustrations.

Design an animated Installation Process timeline covering **Consultation, Site Inspection, Engineering Design, Manufacturing, Quality Testing, Installation, Commissioning, Safety Inspection, Final Delivery, and Annual Maintenance Support**.

Create a dedicated Maintenance & AMC section describing Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annual, and Emergency Maintenance plans, along with benefits such as increased equipment life, reduced downtime, improved safety, lower repair costs, and better performance.

Display completed projects in a modern filterable gallery categorized into **Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Hospital, Hotel, Mall, and Government Projects**. Each project card should include images, location, elevator type, number of floors, capacity, and completion details.

Include premium customer testimonials, certification badges (ISO, Safety Standards, Government Approvals, Industry Certifications), an interactive FAQ section, and a modern contact section featuring a contact form, office address, phone numbers, email, Google Maps integration, business hours, and emergency contact information.

The footer should contain quick links for Company, Products, Services, Industries, Projects, Blog, Careers, Contact, Newsletter Subscription, Social Media Links, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions.

Use **Framer Motion** to add smooth animations such as fade-up effects, hero text reveals, hover lift animations, image zoom effects, counter animations, timeline animations, and page transitions. Keep animations subtle, elegant, and performance-friendly.

Use high-quality royalty-free images related to **glass elevators, luxury buildings, elevator installation, maintenance engineers, manufacturing facilities, commercial projects, and modern architecture**. Avoid generic stock photos.

Ensure the website is **fully responsive**, optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Implement lazy loading, SEO optimization, semantic HTML, accessibility (WCAG), structured data (Schema.org), optimized images, and fast loading performance.

Most importantly, recreate the **premium spacing, alignment, navigation layout, typography scale, and overall visual hierarchy** similar to the uploaded reference screenshots. The website should feel luxurious, spacious, elegant, and trustworthy, making Hestra Elevators appear as a leading global elevator engineering company rather than a standard business website.
